Challenge Rules

Follow fair play rules to keep the tournament genuine.

Submission Rules


1. One workout submission per day.

2. Task must be submitted with correct date.

3. Fake proof may lead to rejection.

4. Proof photo/link is recommended.

Verification Rules


1. Own task cannot be verified.

2. Two different members must approve.

3. Same member cannot verify twice.

4. Two rejections can reject the task.

Point System


Valid gym day: 10 points

30+ minutes workout: +5 points

60+ minutes workout: +10 points

Verification: +2 points
